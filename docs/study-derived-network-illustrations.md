# Study-derived gene-set screen beyond Hallmarks

## Outcome

Searched a frozen catalog of **8,041 human-mapped signatures** and screened **402 selected signatures from 222 publications**. Of these, 396 had at least one gene represented in the human GO-CAM network; six had none. None of the 396 tested signatures passed within-set BY FDR 0.05 at 1,999 draws. The ten strongest eligible study-derived signatures were rerun at 19,999 draws with seeds 0, 1 and 2. **0 of the ten selected neighborhoods passed BY 0.05 across all three seeds.**

This is an illustration search using published curated gene lists, not a reanalysis of raw expression data or a validation study. The results are substantially weaker than the constructed Hallmark examples. The score ranges below describe the **same seed-0-selected neighborhood** across seeds, rather than switching neighborhoods to report the best score from each run.

## Provenance audit

The broad collections also contain constructed signatures. We therefore do not treat collection membership as proof of a single experiment.

- **DANG_MYC_TARGETS_UP** was removed before scoring because it compiles a MYC target database. It is not one experimental contrast.
- **FOROUTAN_PRODRANK_TGFB_EMT_UP/DN** explicitly combine multiple datasets by meta-analysis. They remain in the full search results, but were excluded from experimental-illustration confirmation even though the UP set had the best initial score.
- **GSE3039** is a mouse cell-type experiment, confirmed by its [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE3039). The library supplies human-mapped genes, which were tested on the human GO-CAM graph. No new orthology inference was performed here.
- **LU_EZH2_TARGETS_DN** was checked against the [original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2923653/) and [GSE20381](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE20381). The accession describes human arrays with four control-siRNA and four EZH2-siRNA samples. The curated signature cites supplemental Figure S5J/personal communication.
- **TSUNODA_CISPLATIN_RESISTANCE_DN** describes genes reduced in resistant cells, but its MSigDB exact-source field says “Upregulated genes.” The signature is retained with that discrepancy noted; check the original table orientation before making a direction-specific interpretation.
- **TGFB_UP.V1_UP** combines four cell lines within one original TGF-beta experiment. **ESC_V6.5_UP_LATE.V1_UP** pools late differentiation time points. These are more specific than Hallmarks but still aggregated contrasts.
- The remaining source descriptions and qualifications are recorded in [provenance-audit.json](https://github.com/geneontology/amigo3-prototype/releases/download/perturbation-screen-2026-10/perturbation-provenance-audit.json). Detailed original-study checks focused on the leading examples; the full 402-set panel was not individually rederived or fully audited.

## Selection and statistical settings

The source was MyGeneset, reporting MSigDB 2026.1. Its queried catalog contained 2,633 CGP, 5,219 C7 and 189 C6 human-taxonomy records. This is a subset of the broader MSigDB resource, and the screen is a study-balanced sample of that catalog, not an exhaustive test of every available set. [MSigDB collection documentation](https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb/collection_details.jsp) describes the publication-derived provenance of CGP and the separate signature collections.

Before scoring, candidates required a publication ID, 20–1,000 source members and an `_UP` or `_DN` suffix. Publications and contrasts were ordered by a fixed SHA-256 key; one contrast was selected per publication within each collection, preserving both directions where available. Quotas were 240 CGP, 120 C7 and 40 C6 signatures. SANA IFNG and FRASOR estradiol were added as prespecified, previously bundled experimental anchors, yielding 402 signatures. All 402 have publication identifiers and 232 have GEO/accession metadata. The source catalog, selected inputs and selection rule are frozen below.

All analyses used the same 1,631-gene, 2,293-edge human graph, all 741 local PageRank neighborhoods, size bounds 2–1,000, enriched connectivity and the default connected-network background. Up/down refers to expression in the source contrast; both directions were tested for excess network connectivity. No neighborhoods, genes or background bounds were tuned to improve a particular result. Confirmation chose the strongest non-meta-analysis signatures with at most two per publication.

Coverage is limited: the median signature had 17 eligible network genes, and the median eligible/input fraction was 12.6%. Original assay backgrounds were not reconstructed; default-network-background results are therefore exploratory rather than matched-assay inference.

BY correction covers the 741 neighborhoods within each gene-set analysis, **not the search across 402 signatures**. Different random seeds assess Monte Carlo variability, not independent biological replication. The smallest Monte Carlo p-value is 0.0005 at 1,999 draws and 0.00005 at 19,999; many leading results reach this floor. Thus small adjusted values and their ordering can remain sensitive to sampling precision.

No-network-input cases: `HOFT_CD4_POSITIVE_ALPHA_BETA_MEMORY_T_CELL_BCG_VACCINE_AGE_18_45YO_56D_TOP_100_DEG_AFTER_IN_VITRO_RE_STIMULATION_UP`, `KORKOLA_EMBRYONIC_CARCINOMA_VS_SEMINOMA_UP`, `MAHADEVAN_IMATINIB_RESISTANCE_UP`, `RORIE_TARGETS_OF_EWSR1_FLI1_FUSION_DN`, `WEBER_METHYLATED_HCP_IN_SPERM_UP`, `WU_ALZHEIMER_DISEASE_DN`. These six were untestable and were not silently discarded from the inventory.
